How To Play

Guess all countries matching the daily challenge.
- Guess all matching countries to win the daily game.
- After 5 wrong guesses, the game is over.
- Use hints if needed when stuck.
- Continents hint reveals where missing countries are located.
- The capital city determines which continent a country belongs to (e.g. Turkey is in Asia, Russia is in Europe).
- Initials hint reveals the starting letter of missing countries.
- A brand new challenge unlocks every single day.
- Note: Denmark shares a land border with Canada (Greenland), and France shares a border with the Netherlands (Saint Martin).
